# KTD — Unleash the Dragon

## Direção inédita

Esta faixa é uma nova composição-manifesto, distinta das provas boom bap e trap anteriores. O centro criativo é a libertação sem pedido de licença: KTD rompe a contenção, assume a própria voz e transforma pressão em movimento. A sonoridade deve usar trap/rap de alta energia, com subgrave 808, bateria seca e recortada, hi-hats rápidos, synths metálicos e um refrão de chamada e resposta que seja agressivo, memorável e não pop.

O hook central é **“Unleash the dragon and fuck it”**. A expressão é usada como gesto de autonomia artística e ruptura com expectativas, não como ameaça real. A faixa deve permanecer original, sem copiar melodias, samples, letras, flows ou performances reconhecíveis.

## Letra original

### Intro

No leash on the fire. No permission for the storm.

### Verse 1

I kept the fuse in my teeth, now the whole wire glows,
seven shadows on my back where the pressure grows.
They built a little box and called it a throne,
I cracked the little box and I carried my own.
No borrowed myth, no rented name, no counterfeit crown,
I learned how to rise when the room sat down.
I came from the silence with a pulse in the drum,
when the lock says wait, I say watch what I become.

### Pre-hook

Count it: one breath, two hands, three strikes on the floor.
Four walls, five doubts, six chains — not anymore.

### Hook

Unleash the dragon and fuck it, let the whole sky split.
I was born with the pressure, now I weaponize the grit.
Unleash the dragon and fuck it, no leash, no counterfeit.
If the world wants a smaller me, the world can deal with it.

### Verse 2

I do not need applause from a room that never listened,
I turn the static into scripture, every syllable glistening.
Fast lane, brake check, then I snap into the pocket,
heart full of thunder, let the kick drum knock it.
I have pain in the archive and gold in the scar,
I have truth in the mouth and a map to the stars.
Call it reckless, call it wrong, call it too damn vivid,
I would rather be a problem than a life half-hidden.

### Break

Say my name low. Now say it like a warning to the silence.
I am not asking for the door. I am changing the climate.

### Final hook

Unleash the dragon and fuck it, let the whole sky split.
Seven heads in the storm and every one of them commits.
Unleash the dragon and fuck it, no leash, no counterfeit.
Kháirus the Dragon — I was built to move through it.

Letra original preparada para geração e aprovação humana. A nova faixa não deve reutilizar a estrutura ou o material sonoro das provas anteriores.
