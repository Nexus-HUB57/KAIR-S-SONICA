# Playlist de lançamento de KTD — três letras originais

## Direção editorial da playlist

A playlist de estreia foi desenhada como uma trilogia narrativa: **identidade**, **família** e **consciência**. Cada faixa tem um hook curto, uma frase visualmente legível em legenda e uma unidade de 15–30 segundos que pode ser performada em vídeo vertical. Isso segue a lógica de descoberta e participação observada em plataformas de vídeo curto, sem prometer viralização e sem copiar áudio ou estrutura de terceiros. A pesquisa de contexto está em [`docs/ktd-launch-research.md`](ktd-launch-research.md).

As letras são originais e devem ser tratadas como material de composição. A gravação futura precisa preservar a referência vocal oficial `assets/audio/kairos-rapid-rap-flow-demo-en-v3.mp3`, mas não deve clonar ou imitar intérpretes reais.

# 01 — DRAGON IN THE MIRROR

### Papel na playlist

Faixa de apresentação e manifesto. O objetivo é apresentar KTD em uma frase: ele não procura uma identidade pronta; ele encara a própria história e entra no palco com ela. A direção sugerida é boom bap moderno com baixo limpo, synth de espelho quebrado e hook de chamada e resposta.

| Parâmetro de escrita | Direção |
| --- | --- |
| Andamento sugerido | 104 BPM |
| Centro tonal sugerido | Fá menor |
| Recorte principal | “I don’t chase the light — I bring it with me.” |
| Hook social | “Who’s that in the mirror? KTD.” |
| Emoção | Autoconfiança ferida que vira presença |

### Lyrics

**[Intro]**

Look me dead in the eye — one storm, two colors.
No mask. No borrowed name.

**[Verse 1]**

I came from the edge where the rent took the dinner,
where a kid learned early that the quiet don’t win ya.
I kept my head low, but the vision stayed tall,
now the whole room bends when I answer the call.
They wanted a version that was easy to market,
I brought every scar and I lit up the target.
Not a saint, not a myth, not a rumor in chrome,
I built this voice from the bricks of my home.

Left eye honey, right eye ice,
fire in the chest but I measure it twice.
Seven claws open where the old fear split,
I don’t chase the light — I bring it with me.

**[Pre-hook]**

Say my name when the bass cuts thin.
Say my name when the doubt walks in.

**[Hook]**

Who’s that in the mirror? KTD.
Who’s that with the pressure? Me.
I don’t chase the light — I bring it with me.
Dragon in the mirror, now the whole world sees.

**[Verse 2]**

I learned from the losses, I studied the flame,
I stopped letting strangers put a lock on my name.
If the past is a blade, then the pen is the handle,
I turn every wound to a road through the scandal.
No rented rebellion, no costume, no pose,
I know what it costs when the family grows.
I was built in the fracture, refined in the rain,
now the rhythm says freedom in a language of pain.

Fast tongue, hard stop, let the pocket get nervous,
I’m not here to perform — I’m here to serve purpose.
If the room wants a monster, I give them the myth,
then I show them the man who refused to be split.

**[Bridge]**

I see the boy in the glass, and he still looks back.
I tell him, “We made it — now stay on the track.”

**[Final Hook]**

Who’s that in the mirror? KTD.
Who’s that with the pressure? Me.
I don’t chase the light — I bring it with me.
Dragon in the mirror, now the whole world sees.

# 02 — SIX NAMES

### Papel na playlist

Faixa emocional e familiar. Ela transforma o sacrifício pelos seis irmãos em um hino de gratidão sem abandonar o rap de impacto. O hook deve funcionar tanto em coro de palco quanto em recorte de vídeo com texto na tela.

| Parâmetro de escrita | Direção |
| --- | --- |
| Andamento sugerido | 96 BPM |
| Centro tonal sugerido | Ré menor |
| Recorte principal | “Six names in my chest, six lights in the dark.” |
| Hook social | “I carried the winter — now we light the dark.” |
| Emoção | Gratidão, responsabilidade e vitória compartilhada |

### Lyrics

**[Intro]**

This one is for the house that held the storm.
Six names. One promise.

**[Verse 1]**

I was young with a job and a world on my shoulder,

Every late-night shift put a brick in the road,

I did not have wings, so I carried the weather,
kept six dreams warm when the cold came together.
If you hear my voice, hear the family in the spark,

**[Hook]**

If I rise, we rise, that’s the shape of the art.

**[Verse 2]**

I missed out on years, but I never missed the reason,

They see the cars now, they don’t see the repairs,

I don’t flex for the camera, I answer the doubt,

Now the bass hits hard like a door kicked open,

I’m not a solo victory, I’m a bridge with a spark,

**[Bridge]**

If you come from the bottom, put your hand to the sky.
If they counted you out, let them watch you multiply.
No one gets left in the story we start.
Six names in my chest, six flames in one heart.

**[Final Hook]**

Six names in my chest, six lights in the dark.
I carried the winter — now we light the dark.
If I rise, we rise, that’s the shape of the art.

# 03 — GOLDEN SCARS

### Papel na playlist

Faixa de consciência pública. O objetivo é colocar KTD diante de sistemas de exclusão sem perder humanidade. O arranjo sugerido é trap/boom bap híbrido, com subgrave, hi-hats em rajadas, espaço seco para a voz e ponte quase falada.

| Parâmetro de escrita | Direção |
| --- | --- |
| Andamento sugerido | 110 BPM |
| Centro tonal sugerido | Dó sustenido menor |
| Recorte principal | “They want the shine, not the scars — I bring both.” |
| Hook social | “Bring the truth, bring the scars.” |
| Emoção | Indignação lúcida e responsabilidade coletiva |

### Lyrics

**[Intro]**

They love the glow when they can crop the story.
I’m bringing the whole frame.

**[Verse 1]**

They want the shine, not the scars — I bring both.
They want the quote, not the years that it wrote.
They want a clean image, a safe little villain,
I bring the full truth and the room starts shifting.
I know what a closed door sounds like at midnight,
know what a wrong name does under blue lights.
But I refuse to turn pain into a throne,
I build a wider table from the splinters I own.

No hate in the message, no leash on the question,
I aim at the system, then examine my reflection.
If the future is a fire, let the children breathe,
we can break the old pattern without breaking the weak.

**[Hook]**

Bring the truth, bring the scars.
Bring the night into the stars.
They want the shine, not the scars — I bring both.
KTD in the frame, let the whole world know.

**[Verse 2]**

I’m against the silence that protects the abuser,
against every headline that reduces the loser.
Against the cheap pride that makes cruelty fashion,
against turning a wound into somebody’s caption.
I speak for the streets but I don’t own their stories,
I leave room for the names, for the people before me.
My faith is a compass, my anger is measured,
my freedom means more when the next one is protected.

Fast with the facts, then I breathe through the sorrow,
plant one seed in the beat for a better tomorrow.
No promise of perfection, no polished disguise,
just a voice with a purpose and a fire in its eyes.

**[Bridge]**

If you see me rise, don’t mistake it for distance.
I’m carrying the ones who were erased from existence.
The dragon is loud, but the mission is clear:
turn pressure to power, make the silence hear.

**[Final Hook]**

Bring the truth, bring the scars.
Bring the night into the stars.
They want the shine, not the scars — I bring both.
KTD in the frame, let the whole world know.

## Regras de produção para as três faixas

As três letras devem ser gravadas com a referência vocal oficial de KTD como âncora de identidade, sem clonar intérpretes reais. Cada single deve ter uma introdução curta, um hook reconhecível antes dos primeiros 40 segundos, uma unidade vertical de 15–30 segundos e uma versão instrumental/acapella para participação da comunidade. A aprovação final precisa considerar interpretação, pocket, inteligibilidade, originalidade, ausência de artefatos e coerência com a história do artista.
