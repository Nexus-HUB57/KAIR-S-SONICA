## [Intro — falado, boom bap seco]
Never left my city. Twenty-something years, same ten blocks.
Then one phone call put me on a plane… to the desert of lights.

## [Verse 1 — boom bap, narrativo]
I seen lights on a screen, never lights on a strip,
First night in a suite with a view off the cliff of it,
Hundred-fifty on the dash, foreign, don't know how to park it,
Valet laughin' at the kid who never left the projects.
Five-star sheets, I was scared to wrinkle 'em,
Room service menu, I was reading like a scripture, man,
My brothers with me — the real ones, not the new ones,
Every bottle that we popped was a year that we got through 'em.

## [Pre-Chorus]
They say what happens here… stays here…
So I'm only giving you the half, yeah —

## [Chorus — drop festa]
What happens in Vegas — stays in Vegas!
Neon on my skin like the city tryna claim us,
Poor kid in the penthouse, money on the table,
Spend it like it's nothin' 'cause the struggle made me able!
What happens in Vegas — stays in Vegas!
Beautiful strangers and my brothers, that's the cadence,
Strippers on the pole, hundred-dollar confetti —
I was never 'posed to be here… so I'm living in it heavy!

## [Verse 2 — reflexivo]
Next morning, quiet, city humming like a secret,
Checked my pockets — more than my father made a season,
And I felt a little guilty, felt a little golden,
Every blessing hit different when your back's been holding.
I ain't lose who I was in the neon and the liquor,
Vegas showed me heaven got a cover charge, I'm witnessing,
Cut the beat. Let it breathe.
