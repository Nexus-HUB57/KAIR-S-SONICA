## [Chorus]
I don't climb and cut the rope behind me,
Every rung I touched, I leave it timed, see —
Somebody held the ladder when the ground was all I knew,
So I hold the ladder steady, 'cause the next one might be you.

## [Verse 1]
I remember concrete mornings, pockets light as my patience,
Mama's prayers in the hallway, that's my first congregation,
Seen a king fall to pieces 'cause nobody showed the blueprint,
Now I draw it in the margins so the young ones read between it.
I ain't never been a savior, I'm a witness with a work ethic,
Turned the weight into a barbell, every setback was a metric,
They ask me how I made it — I say somebody showed the door,
And I promised at the threshold I would never block the floor.

## [Verse 2]
Now my name travel further than my feet ever walked,
But the measure of a man is in the ladder that he caught,
Not the height of where he standing — it's the hand he leave extended,
Every ceiling that I shattered is a floor that I amended.
Six names on my back, yeah they heavy but they hold me,
Every scar turned to gold, now the gold turn to homely —
A table with a seat that I set before I eat,
'Cause the dream don't mean a thing if it don't repeat.

## [Bridge — half-time]
Hold it steady… hold it steady…
The ladder only works if somebody holds it steady.

## [Outro]
Hold it steady.
