# Single 16 — HE DIED KNEELING — Letra v1 (EN)

- Artista: Kháirus the Dragon (KTD)
- Status: letra v1 — prova 1 em geração
- Tema: KTD perde o melhor amigo, morto por uma gangue rival na noite em que ele deixaria o crime para pedir Angel em casamento — alianças trocadas pelas armas, traído por quem fingiu aceitar sua saída. Morto ajoelhado, aos pés do amor, na praça onde se conheceram.
- Direção: melódica mas com batida inspiradora e singular; tomada de ira e poesia; refrão construído para inundar reels/TikTok/Instagram
- Regra de continuidade: sem repetir metáforas centrais anteriores (espelho, nomes, cicatrizes, assento, porta, rio, silêncio, coroa, escada, neon de Vegas)

## [Intro — spoken]
He called me that night... "I traded my guns for two rings."

## [Chorus — melodic, sung]
He died kneeling — but he died a man,
Rings in his hand, love in his plan.
They moved like cowards, he stood like a king,
He died kneeling — but he died a man.

## [Verse 1]
He loved the fast life, but he loved her more,
Angel from the forbidden side of the war,
Called me up, said "brother, tonight I'm clean,
I traded every pistol for a wedding ring."
Told 'em to their face, "I'm leaving the life,"
Walked in, walked out, head high — no strife,
They shook his hand, smiled, said "it's all love,"
Then whispered to the dark: "he's unarmed... he's alone."

## [Pre-Chorus]
"Reserve the suit, padrinho — you stand at the altar with me."
That was the last time my brother ever called me.

## [Chorus]

## [Verse 2]
Same square where he met her, knees on the stone,
Rings in his palm, 'bout to make her his own,
They came from the shadows, no honor, no face,
He never shot a soul in the back — disgrace.
Rest in peace, my brother, you were a man,
Tried to leave a world that never gave you a chance,
Wanted clean hands — no guns, just proof of love,
A decision, an honor they'll never know the meaning of.

## [Bridge — half-time, quase falado]
Cowards... you were cowards...
He died kneeling... but he died a man.

## [Chorus final — voz em camadas, coral abrindo]

## [Outro]
Rings, not rounds. That's how a king falls.
