# SINGLE #18 — THE CHAIN CAME HOME

**Artista:** KTD — Kháirus The Dragon  
**Status:** novo proof narrativo; substitui o conceito anterior do Single 18.  
**Formato:** storytelling de rap cinematográfico, ficcionalizado e não instrucional.

## Conceito

“The Chain Came Home” é uma crônica de rivalidade, memória e consequência. O cordão de ouro que KTD recebeu do pai não é tratado como luxo: é um objeto de origem, afeto e identidade. O conflito começa quando uma disputa amorosa e uma briga pública alimentam a humilhação entre dois rappers. O rival manda uma intermediária roubar o cordão e aparece em um show usando-o como troféu, transformando uma lembrança familiar em provocação pública.

KTD recupera o cordão, mas a rivalidade escala. Em uma emboscada, tiros atingem o carro e ferem KTD no braço e no ombro. A canção não descreve a mecânica do ataque nem celebra a retaliação; concentra-se no choque, na sobrevivência e no peso de carregar uma memória que quase foi transformada em espetáculo.

No final, KTD descobre que o rival morreu pouco depois em um acidente de moto contra uma árvore. O sarcasmo é seco e amargo: a natureza não vingou ninguém, apenas permaneceu de pé enquanto homens confundiam troféu com valor. KTD não comemora a morte; ele aponta a ironia de alguém que desrespeitou tudo — pessoas, memória e natureza — e terminou diante de uma árvore que não podia ser intimidada.

### [Intro — The Chain]

K-T-D, late night, gold cold against my chest,
My father gave me more than metal, he gave me what was left.
Everybody knew the weight, everybody knew the name,
A family memory shining through the smoke and fame.

She was his ex, but she was with me; that was enough to start the war,
Old disagreements, old bad blood, then we met outside the door.
Two teeth gone, nose broken, pride bleeding on the floor,
He never lost the lesson — he only wanted more.

We traded shots in records, traded names across the town,
Every line became a match, every room became a battleground.
But the chain was not a prop, not a prize for any show,
It was my father’s hand around my neck before he let me go.

### [Pre-Chorus — The Theft]

Then he sent a woman with a smile and a borrowed plan,
She lifted what he knew I loved and placed it in his hand.
He wore it under spotlights like he conquered what I was,
A stolen piece of my father turned into applause.

The chain came home, but the night got colder,
Gold on my skin, weight on my shoulder.
You can steal the shine, you can stage the scene,
But you cannot own what the memory means.

The chain came home, but the debt stayed near,
Some men chase a symbol till they disappear.

I kept the wheel straight, kept the engine under breath,
Not because I was fearless — I was choosing life over death.
The chain was in the console, my father’s gold beside the scar,
I learned how fast a feud can turn a name into a car.

Hospital white, blue gloves, blood drying on the sleeve,
The doctor said I made it; I said I still had things to leave.
The songs were never worth a mother waiting by the phone,

Then word came quiet: he was gone before the morning,
Motorcycle, wet road, one tree, no warning.

He made a trophy out of theft; the tree kept all its roots.

He hated anything that would not bow beneath his name,

So here is my dark joke, let the silence hear it through:
The tree did not chase him, it simply stayed where trees do.

No victory in a coffin, no crown inside the soil,

But you cannot own what the memory means.
KTD still standing, no trophy, no disguise,
I carry what my father gave me under open skies.
The chain came home, the night released,
Nature kept her silence, and I chose my peace.

### [Outro — KTD Remembers]
