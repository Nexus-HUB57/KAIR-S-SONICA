# Single 16 — HE DIED KNEELING — Letra v2 (EN)

- **Artista:** Kháirus the Dragon (KTD)
- **Status:** `DRAFT` — letra v2 para prova musical; não é master nem autorização de lançamento
- **Função no arco:** luto, honra e traição; abre o pós-epílogo com uma memória que transforma ira em testemunho
- **Idioma oficial de composição:** inglês
- **Conceito:** o melhor amigo de KTD decide abandonar a vida de crime para pedir Angel em casamento. Ele troca as armas por alianças, avisa a própria gangue com respeito e é entregue desarmado. Na praça onde conheceu Angel, morre ajoelhado diante do amor que escolhera. A canção condena a covardia e celebra a tentativa de viver com as mãos limpas.
- **Direção vocal:** verso narrativo em rap frontal, médio-grave, com double-time controlado; refrão melódico em forma de hino, agressivo sem virar grito indistinto; ponte quase falada, com desprezo contido.
- **Regra de continuidade:** a violência é contexto de perda e traição, nunca glamour, tutorial, ameaça real ou destino desejável. Não há descrição gráfica da morte.

## [Intro — spoken, close and breath-held]

He called me that night — two rings in his palm.
He said, “Brother, I traded my weapons for a wedding plan.”
I told him, “Say it again.”
He said, “My life of crime ends tonight.”

## [Verse 1 — narrative rap, clipped pocket]

He loved the fast life, but he loved her more,
Angel lived across the line, in the place we called forbidden ground.
One side of the city taught us who to hate,
She taught him that a heart can still walk out the gate.

He called me from a room with the curtains drawn,
Said, “KTD, the old life is gone by dawn.”
Two small circles shining where the pistols used to lay,
He traded every weapon for a different kind of faith.

“I’m going to tell them clean — no running, no disguise.
I’ll walk in as their brother and walk out with my eyes high.”
He told the crew the truth: “I’m leaving this behind.”
They shook his hand and smiled like they respected his decision.

But respect was only theater, the silence hid the plan,
A whisper crossed the alley: “He’ll be there with empty hands.”
They marked the hour, marked the corner, marked the place,
Then sent a lie dressed up as peace into his face.

## [Pre-Chorus — rising, low doubles]

Then my phone lit up: “Brother, save the suit.
You’re my best man at the altar — I want you in the room.”
I heard the future breathing underneath his voice.
That was the last call before the cowards made their choice.

## [Verse 2 — pressure increases, controlled double-time]

Same square where they first met, wet stone under the light,
Angel standing in the center of the future he had written for the night.
He dropped to one knee, opened up his hand,
Two rings, one question, one clean and fearless plan.

Then the dark broke open — no warning, no face,
Cowards never carry honor when they enter someone’s space.
He never turned a barrel on a brother’s back,
Never called betrayal justice, never made a coward’s pact.

He fell where love was waiting, not where glory gets displayed,
The promise still inside his hand, the old world in its grave.
I arrived too late with a storm behind my eyes,
Saw a man choose a new life — saw that choice get sacrificed.

So rest easy, my brother, you were more than what you did,
You tried to leave a violent world before it swallowed what you built.
You wanted clean hands, no weapons, no disguise,
Just Angel, just a future, just the truth behind your eyes.

but you cannot make honor mean what you mean.
