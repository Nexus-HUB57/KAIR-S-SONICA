# BUILD THE DOOR BEHIND ME — official lyric v1

## Intro — low voice, room tone

I know what a closed door sounds like.  
I know what a “not yet” costs.  
I walked through fire with my name intact.  
Now watch what I build from the loss.

## Verse 1 — pressure turned into structure

I used to count the distance from the floor to the light,  
count every locked gate that was dressed up as right.  
They said, “If you make it, then the system must work,”  
but a crown on one head does not erase the dirt.

I remember borrowed hours, cracked screens, late trains,  
the kind of small beginnings that they never put in frames.  
I remember every room that made me prove I belonged,  
every “wait outside” that became a harder song.

I carried my mother’s lessons, carried my father’s name,  
carried six living reasons through the weather and the flames.  
I learned a door is useless if it opens for one,  
so I’m building something wide enough for everyone.

No perfect road, no rescue scene, no handout from above,  
just a discipline that turned the pressure into blood.  
I did not climb to tell the bottom that it should disappear.  
I climbed to make the route visible from here.

## Pre-chorus — lift through the last bar

If I found a way through fire, I will mark it in the ground.  
If the room was built for silence, we will make the walls resound.  
I am not the end of the story, I am proof the page can bend.  
When I build the door behind me, let the next one walk right in.

## Chorus — open, melodic, collective response

I built the door behind me — come on in.  
I turned the weight I carried into wind.  
I don’t climb alone, I bring the whole line.  
What they called impossible is becoming mine.

No borrowed crown, no gatekeeper’s sign.  
If I found a little light, then the light is yours and mine.  
I built the door behind me — let the next one walk right in.  
I don’t rise just to escape. I rise so we can begin.

## Verse 2 — the victory becomes access

Now they want the photograph, want the polished elevation,  
want to call the outcome proof of equal opportunity.  
But I know who was missing when the camera found the stage,  
know how many gifted lives got trapped outside the page.

So I put a mic in the hand they told to stay quiet,  
put a book on the table, put a future beside it.  
I share the map, not because the road is clean,  
but because nobody should have to guess what the signs mean.

For the kid with the night shift and a dream after work,  
for the one with the sharp mind that the gate called a threat,  
for the artist with a voice but no room to rehearse,  
for the family making miracles from the last in the purse.

I am not promising easy, I am promising a frame,  
a place to bring the struggle without hiding the name.  
If the ladder bends, we brace it; if the bridge breaks, we mend.  
I came up through the pressure — now the pressure makes room again.

## Bridge — half-time, honest and expansive

So take this name, take this spark, take the lesson from the flame.  
Take the room I had to fight for; you do not owe them shame.  
If they ask who gave you permission, tell them: “We became the key.”  
The door is not behind me now — it is opening through me.

## Performance direction

The intro should feel close and grounded, as if KTD is speaking from the room where the struggle began. Verse 1 should use a firm narrative pocket with short bursts of double-time on “borrowed hours, cracked screens, late trains.” The first pre-chorus should widen the harmony without rushing the vocal. The chorus must be memorable, forceful and communal, with the lead centered and the choir answering “come on in” and “we can begin.”

Verse 2 should sound more assured but not triumphant in a careless way; the performance must remember the people who were excluded from the photograph. The bridge should strip back to half-time, expose the remaining scars and then rise into the final chorus. The final choir should feel like a door opening for a crowd, not like a solo victory lap.

## Editorial guardrails

The lyric is original and uses broad social experience rather than naming an unverified institution, party, person or specific organization. It does not promise that individual effort solves structural inequality. KTD’s identity is expressed through narrative clarity, controlled aggression, melodic conviction and collective purpose, without imitating a real artist or reproducing an existing lyric, melody, sample or arrangement.
