# I WON'T WASTE THIS LIFE — production brief v1

**Single:** 11
**Artist:** KTD / Kháirus the Dragon
**Status:** first musical proof; lyric and chorus candidates; not approved
**Language:** English
**Tempo:** 102 BPM
**Key:** F-sharp minor
**Target duration:** approximately 2:40–2:55
**Audio target:** WAV PCM 24-bit, 44.1 kHz, stereo; MP3 320 kbps

## Creative direction

Create one cohesive original rap-rock / nu metal melodic song for KTD. The emotional arc is survival becoming presence: KTD remembers poverty, loss, family pressure and closed doors, then decides not to waste the life he fought to keep. The song must feel commercial, global and emotionally immediate without imitating any real artist, band, song, melody, lyric, voice, riff or recognizable arrangement.

Use the official KTD identity: medium-low frontal male rap vocal, warm chest resonance, slight rasp, clear diction, controlled aggression, internal rhymes, short double-time bursts, half-time resets and pauses of impact. Do not use a celebrity imitation or biometric clone. Keep the vocal intelligible and central.

## Sonic blueprint

Use a dark but uplifting F-sharp-minor foundation at 102 BPM. Verse loop: F#m–Dmaj7–A–E. Pre-chorus: D–E–C#sus4–C#7. Chorus: A–E–F#m–D. Bridge: Bm–D–A–E. Build a deep, controlled kick, a marked snare, a thick mono-centered bass with audible harmonic saturation, palm-muted low guitars in the verses, wide original guitars in the chorus, and a restrained syncopated percussion layer that dialogues with global rap/funk rhythms without copying a recognizable pattern.

Keep the verse relatively sparse so KTD owns the center. Increase density and width at the pre-chorus. Let the chorus open with full guitars, bass, drums, lead vocal, doubles and a collective response on “I won’t waste this life.” Strip the bridge to half-time with a more exposed vocal, then return to the final chorus with maximum emotional width rather than only more loudness.

Mix for punch and translation: deep but controlled low end, audible kick and snare on mobile speakers, vocal-first center image, wide guitars and choruses without phase collapse, short dark room in verses, larger plate or hall only in chorus and bridge, subtle vocal distortion as texture, no crushed master, no clipping and no excessive sibilance. Preserve dynamic contrast between verse, chorus and bridge.

## Candidate lyric guide

[Intro — low, cinematic]

I used to count the ceiling cracks while the whole block slept,
learned to hide the fear behind the promises I kept.

[Verse 1 — clipped narrative rap]

I carried empty rooms, bad news, and a name they tried to bend,
kept walking through the damage like the damage was not the end.
I saw the strong go silent, saw the good get buried deep,
saw a family lose its balance while the city went to sleep.
I gave my younger years to every fire at the door,
now I’m standing in the daylight — I don’t owe the dark no more.

[Pre-chorus — rising, sustained phrases]

I could let the old nights keep a room inside my head,
I could feed the same old fire every time I go to bed.
But the years they tried to take from me are breathing in my chest —
if I’m still here, then I’m not finished; I’m not finished yet.

[Chorus — wide melodic anthem]

I won’t waste this life.
Not one more night, not one more time.
Turned every loss into a reason to rise.
I won’t waste this life.
The dark don’t get what’s left of mine.
If I got breath in my chest, I got a reason to shine.

[Performance note]

The final words and any additional verses may be completed musically by the generation, but the title hook must remain exactly recognizable. Do not replace the approved development hook with a different chorus concept. Keep the song cohesive and suitable for a later human lyric lock.

## Generation guardrails

Avoid generic pop-rock, EDM drops, constant screaming, thin bass, over-bright hi-hats, excessive guitar density, novelty percussion, comedy, triumphal wealth-flexing, threats, graphic violence, hate speech, or any identifiable imitation of a real performer. The song should be inspiring without becoming childish, aggressive without sounding like a real-world threat, and cinematic without losing the KTD rap pocket.
