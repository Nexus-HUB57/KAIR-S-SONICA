## Performance direction

The intro should be low, close and cinematic, with KTD sounding observant rather than amazed. Verse 1 should use a clipped, frontal rap delivery with deliberate pauses around the numerical escalation. The vocal should feel like a man examining the new world that surrounds him, not like a generic celebration of wealth.

The first pre-chorus must tighten the phrasing and introduce low doubles. The chorus should open into a wide nu-metal/rap-rock impact with a simple melody, strong unison layers and a clear crowd-response shape. The title line must be instantly intelligible on the first listen and should carry the viral excerpt.

Verse 2 should move faster and sound more suspicious. Anger should arrive through consonant attack, rhythmic precision and controlled distortion rather than constant screaming. The bridge must strip the arrangement into a half-time confession about health, time, mortality and legacy before the final chorus returns at maximum width.

The final performance should feel victorious but lucid. KTD is not begging for approval and is not threatening anyone. He is drawing a boundary, refusing hypocrisy and declaring that his identity survived the money, the fame and the disappearing lights.

## Editorial guardrails

This is a first-person artistic narrative. The references to cars, mansions, women and financial digits are symbolic markers of public status and are not claims about real-world assets. The profanity is intentional but should remain rhythmically purposeful. The song must remain original and must not imitate any real artist, voice, lyric, melody, sample or recognizable arrangement from the supplied reference.
