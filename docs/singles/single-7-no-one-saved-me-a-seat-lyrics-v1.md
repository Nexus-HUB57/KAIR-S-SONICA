## Intro — pressão baixa, voz próxima

They told me, “Pick a future,” like the future came in rows.  
Like a number on a paper tells a child where he goes.  
I watched the chosen names rise while the rest were told to wait.  
But a dream can turn to muscle when it’s carrying its own weight.

## Verse 1 — pressão social e portas fechadas

They put a college brochure in a parent’s tired hand,  
Said, “Your child needs a degree if he wants to understand.”  
But the fee sits like a mountain, and the deadline moves like smoke,  
While the ones with names already known get another open door.  
They call it equal access when the distance isn’t fair,  
When one kid has a quiet room and one is fighting for a chair.  
They say, “Work harder,” like the hours come supplied,  
Like the rent won’t take the paycheck, like the family won’t divide.  
I filled out every form with a future in the margins,  
Read the fine print twice, learned the language of the charges.  
Every “not selected” sounded personal and cold,  
Every “try again next year” made the waiting room grow old.  
They wanted proof I was exceptional before they gave me room,  
Wanted flowers from the seed while they kept me from the bloom.  
So I stopped asking permission from a system built to choose,  
If the gate was made for others, I had nothing left to lose.

## Verse 2 — a chance mínima vira caminho

It could be one old laptop with a crack across the screen,  
One late-night library, one borrowed hour to dream.  
One person saying, “Keep going,” when the room says, “Know your place,”  
One small key turned in the lock, one step into the race.  
I studied through the fatigue, worked around the doubt,  
Learned a closed equation is a door you can work out.  
I carried every failure like a brick beneath my arm,  
Built a staircase out of pressure, made the damage do no harm.  
They never saw the mornings when the confidence was gone,  
Only saw the final picture, asked me how I got this far.  
I got here through the nights when nobody replied,  
Through the “almost” and the “not yet,” through the urge to run and hide.  
I did not need a miracle; I needed one real chance,  
Then discipline became the rhythm and the rhythm made me advance.  
Now the ones who called it fantasy can watch the table fill,  
I didn’t wait to be selected — I selected my own will.

## Direção de performance

Os versos devem começar em tom próximo, firme e observador, como quem denuncia uma regra social que aprendeu a reconhecer. A indignação cresce pela articulação de palavras como **“chosen,” “equal access,” “not selected,” “permission”** e **“gate.”** No segundo verso, o flow acelera em pequenas rajadas quando KTD enumera cansaço, trabalho, estudo e disciplina. O refrão abre com voz larga e resposta coletiva, mantendo a frase **“No one saved me a seat”** curta, clara e repetível.

A ponte deve reduzir a bateria e oferecer uma verdade sem romantizar a dificuldade. O último refrão transforma a vitória individual em abertura para outros jovens: KTD não está dizendo que todos os obstáculos desapareceram; está dizendo que uma chance pode virar caminho quando encontra disciplina, coragem e comunidade.

A letra critica desigualdade e pressão institucional como experiência social e percepção de exclusão. Não acusa nominalmente governo, partido, universidade ou pessoa específica, nem promete que esforço individual resolve todas as barreiras. A canção permanece autoral e não reproduz letras, melodias, samples ou maneirismos de obras existentes.
